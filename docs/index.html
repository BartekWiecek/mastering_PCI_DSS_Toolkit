WORK WITH THE MATERIALS

What's included

Workbooks

Blank and fictional TechSphere examples for scoping, ownership, controls, decisions and evidence.

Registers & templates

Editable CSV and Word resources, including supplier responsibilities, risk analysis and incident response.

Control navigation

Requirement references, assessment-form membership and a planning calendar. Verify actual applicability separately.

Five practice labs

Explore payment-page security, payment state, segmentation, TLS and a constrained AI tool gateway with synthetic fixtures.

Start here

  1. Extract the full ZIP, keeping its folder structure.
  2. Read toolkit/README.md.
  3. Open the blank workbook for your project or the TechSphere workbook for a worked example.
  4. Follow each lab's instructions in an authorized local environment.

Important

Independent educational material, not an official assessment, endorsement or guarantee of compliance. Never enter live payment data or secrets into these examples. Use the applicable official standard and forms from the PCI SSC Document Library.
