# Implementation Toolkit v1.0.0

**From payment architecture to assessment evidence.**

Put the guidance in *Mastering PCI DSS 4.0.1 Compliance: A Practical Guide for E-commerce Systems — Third Extended Edition* by Bartosz Więcek into practice.

Scope your payment environment, assign responsibilities, plan recurring controls and prepare for an assessment. Start with blank templates, then use the fictional TechSphere example to see how the pieces fit together.

## What's inside

- Two editable workbooks: a blank project workspace and a worked TechSphere example.
- 277 task drafts with proposed owners, timing, acceptance checks and evidence references.
- 508 test-specific evidence-preparation prompts, mapped to active defined-approach testing identifiers.
- 198 calendar entries for planning recurring and event-driven activities.
- Editable registers and templates for scoping, supplier responsibilities, risk analysis, change management and incident response.
- Five hands-on synthetic labs: payment-page security, payment state, network segmentation, TLS acceptance and a constrained AI tool gateway.

## Get started

Download the ZIP, extract the complete folder structure, then open `toolkit/README.md`. Choose the blank workbook or explore the fictional example. Use the applicable official PCI DSS standard and reporting forms to determine actual scope, applicability and testing.

**Reader website:** https://bartekwiecek.github.io/mastering_PCI_DSS_Toolkit/
